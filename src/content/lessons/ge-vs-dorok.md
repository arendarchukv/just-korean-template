Both endings mean "so that", and in many sentences you can use either. The difference is mostly
register: -게 is everyday speech, -도록 sounds more
formal and is common in instructions and writing.

## In everyday speech

잘 들리게 크게 말해 주세요. — Please speak loudly so that I can hear you.

## In instructions

늦지 않도록 일찍 출발하세요. — Leave early so that you are not late.
