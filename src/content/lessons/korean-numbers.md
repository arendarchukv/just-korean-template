Korean has two sets of numbers, and each is used with different counters.

## Native Korean numbers

<span lang="ko">하나, 둘, 셋, 넷, 다섯</span> — for counting things, people and age:
<span lang="ko">사과 두 개</span> (two apples), <span lang="ko">스무 살</span> (twenty years old).

## Sino-Korean numbers

<span lang="ko">일, 이, 삼, 사, 오</span> — for dates, money, minutes and phone numbers:
<span lang="ko">삼월 오일</span> (March 5), <span lang="ko">만 원</span> (10,000 won).
